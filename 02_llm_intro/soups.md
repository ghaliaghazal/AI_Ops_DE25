**1. Pho (Vietnamese Beef Noodle Soup)**  
Description: A deeply aromatic broth simmered with star anise, cinnamon, and cloves, served over rice noodles with fresh herbs and sliced beef.  
Ingredients: Rice noodles, beef bones, star anise, cinnamon, cloves, fish sauce, ginger, onion, thinly sliced beef, Thai basil, lime, bean sprouts.  
Short: Hours of simmering, minutes to serve.  

**2. Tom Yum (Hot and Sour Thai Soup)**  
Description: A vibrant, spicy-sour broth flavored with lemongrass, kaffir lime, and galangal, typically with shrimp or mushrooms.  
Ingredients: Shrimp or mushrooms, lemongrass, kaffir lime leaves, galangal, lime juice, fish sauce, bird’s eye chilies, mushrooms, cilantro.  
Short: Ready in 20 minutes, palate-blasting.  

**3. Miso Shiru (Japanese Miso Soup)**  
Description: A light, umami-rich staple made with fermented miso paste and dashi broth, often with tofu and seaweed.  
Ingredients: Dashi stock, white miso paste, silken tofu, wakame seaweed, green onions.  
Short: 5-minute nourishment.
